      About the data: authors come from Wikidata (people tagged as science fiction writers, or writers with the science fiction genre) who have an English Wikipedia article or an official website. This is not a complete census, so English-language writers are over-represented, and Wikidata tags can be missing or wrong. Birthplaces use the present-day country. "Lived in" uses the recorded country of residence, falling back to citizenship, and is a single country per author. Very small countries and territories may be too small to see on the map and appear only in the ranking.
